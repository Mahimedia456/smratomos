As of November 7, 2026, Atomos Limited has announced the launch of seven new professional reference monitors under its Flanders Scientific (FSI) brand. These monitors are available in two new series: the VP Series (VP270, VP310, VP551, VP651) and the V Series (V270, V310, V550). They utilize QD-OLED panels, color technology, and calibration capabilities similar to FSI's flagship XMP Series but offer a more focused feature set at lower price points. Shipping for these models began in November 2026. ([investors.atomos.com](https://investors.atomos.com/announcements/NwbLEl-flanders-scientific-launches-seven-new-monitors?utm_source=openai))

This strategic move aims to broaden Atomos' market reach by providing high-quality monitoring solutions to production customers seeking more affordable alternatives to flagship displays. The new monitors are priced up to 48% lower than equivalent XMP models, enhancing accessibility for a wider range of users.
